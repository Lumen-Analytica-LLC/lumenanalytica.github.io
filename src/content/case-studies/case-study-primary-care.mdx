## Executive Brief

Disconnected clinical, billing, and finance systems masked revenue leakage, denial drivers, and capacity loss. A unified analytics layer delivered faster collections, higher visit yield, and operational visibility in under 6 months.

## Client Profile

- Industry: Primary Care Medical Practice
- Size: $8.5M annual revenue, 12 providers (8 MDs, 4 NPs/PAs), 6 locations
- Patient Panel: ~18,000 active patients, 65K+ annual visits
- Payer Mix: 45% Commercial, 35% Medicare, 15% Medicaid, 5% Self-Pay
- Tools: Epic EHR + Athenahealth Practice Management + QuickBooks + Excel files
- Back Office Team: 1 practice manager + 3 medical billers + 2 front desk coordinators per location

## What Changed

- Unified clinical, billing, and finance data into one operational model
- Automated visit-to-payment reconciliation and denial root-cause tracking
- Added no-show prediction and preventive outreach signals
- Standardized provider and location performance metrics

## Build Summary

- Visit-to-payment reconciliation model
- Provider productivity and revenue analytics
- Denial tracking with root-cause analysis
- Quality metric automation (MIPS, HEDIS)
